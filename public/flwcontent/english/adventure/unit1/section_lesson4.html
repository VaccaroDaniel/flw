Lesson 4

Hello Song

Children can say Hello/Hi and Goodbye/Bye in a chant or song with gestures.

Children remember greetings through rhythm, gesture, and short repeated lines.

Goodbye line

Goodbye! Bye!

Focus: leaving words

Gesture practice

Wave hello. Wave goodbye.

Focus: meaning through action

Clap the chant

Clap and say hello.

Focus: rhythm practice

Teacher focus

  • Song input: Play/read the Hello Song. Children listen first, then join the repeated words.
  • Action practice: Use the gesture image: wave toward friend for Hello; wave away for Goodbye.
  • Goodbye input: Use the Goodbye Song scene and model Goodbye! Bye!
  • Sing and clap: Children clap the rhythm while saying the target words.
  • Mini-performance: Small groups sing or chant two lines for the class.
