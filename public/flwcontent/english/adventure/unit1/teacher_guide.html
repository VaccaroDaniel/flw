Teacher Guide

English Adventure World - Unit 1: Hello, Friends!

Teaching focus

Use story, gesture, chant, matching, and speaking missions. Do not over-explain grammar. Build confidence from the first English words.

Lesson 1

Hello, Rainbow Gate!

Children can understand and say Hello and Hi with a clear waving gesture.

Teacher focus

  • Warm-up: Show the cover and Rainbow Gate opening scene. Children wave to the characters.
  • Input: Play or read the greetings: Hello! Hi! Link each greeting to a waving action.
  • TPR practice: Children wave big, wave small, wave high, and wave low while saying Hello or Hi.
  • Picture practice: Use the friends waving image. Ask: Who says Hello? Who says Hi?
  • Mini-check: Each child chooses or says Hello/Hi to Leo and receives one star.

Knowledge points

  • EN-PREA1-U01-L01-KP001 Recognize Hello and Hi
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L01-KP002 Say Hello and Hi
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L01-KP003 Use a waving gesture for greeting

Classroom language

Look at Rainbow Gate.Wave and say hello.Listen and point.Say it again.Great! One more time.
Lesson 2

What's Your Name?

Children can ask and answer names using What's your name? and I'm ___.

Teacher focus

  • Review: Quick hello/hi circle. Children greet Leo, Mia, Toto, and Emma.
  • Dialogue input: Show Mia asking Leo. Model the question and answer with gestures.
  • Controlled practice: Children repeat What's your name? and I'm Leo in rhythm.
  • Name card activity: Children read and match Leo, Mia, Toto, Emma name cards.
  • Name Train: Use the train image. One child asks; the next answers; repeat around the group.

Knowledge points

  • EN-PREA1-U01-L02-KP001 Understand What's your name?
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L02-KP002 Say I'm + name
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L02-KP003 Match names to characters

Classroom language

What's your name?I'm Leo.Your turn.Ask your friend.Good speaking!
Lesson 3

Meet My Friends

Children can use the chunk This is ___ to introduce a friend.

Teacher focus

  • Story input: Toto introduces Leo, Mia, and Emma using the three introduction scenes.
  • Chunk practice: Children repeat This is Leo/Mia/Emma. Do not explain grammar.
  • Point and say: Teacher points to a character; children say This is ___. One-word support is allowed for shy learners.
  • Friend match: Use the matching page. Children match name cards, photos, and silhouettes.
  • Mini-check: Each child introduces one character to the class.

Knowledge points

  • EN-PREA1-U01-L03-KP001 Understand This is + name
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L03-KP002 Introduce a friend by name
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L03-KP003 Match friend cards and character pictures

Classroom language

This is Leo.This is Mia.This is Emma.Point to Toto.Match the friend card.
Lesson 4

Hello Song

Children can say Hello/Hi and Goodbye/Bye in a chant or song with gestures.

Teacher focus

  • Song input: Play/read the Hello Song. Children listen first, then join the repeated words.
  • Action practice: Use the gesture image: wave toward friend for Hello; wave away for Goodbye.
  • Goodbye input: Use the Goodbye Song scene and model Goodbye! Bye!
  • Sing and clap: Children clap the rhythm while saying the target words.
  • Mini-performance: Small groups sing or chant two lines for the class.

Knowledge points

  • EN-PREA1-U01-L04-KP001 Recognize Hello versus Goodbye
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L04-KP002 Say Goodbye and Bye
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L04-KP003 Participate in song, clap, and action practice

Classroom language

Sing with me.Clap and say hello.Wave goodbye.Again, please.Wonderful singing!
Lesson 5

Hello Badge Mission

Children complete a final greeting mission and receive the Hello Badge.

Teacher focus

  • Mission setup: Show the Hello Badge Mission cover and explain today's challenge: say hello, say name, choose greetings.
  • Review quiz: Use the choose-the-greeting image. Children choose Hello/Goodbye and Hi/Bye.
  • Speaking mission: Children say Hello! I'm ___. Teacher or system gives feedback.
  • Badge reward: Show the reward image. Give a sticker, star, or digital badge.
  • Reflection: Children say: I can say hello. I can say my name.

Knowledge points

  • EN-PREA1-U01-L05-KP001 Review greetings and names
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L05-KP002 Choose greetings in context
  • EN-PREA1-U01-L05-KP003 Complete a short speaking mission

Classroom language

Are you ready?Choose the greeting.Say your name.Introduce yourself.You did it!

Final badge rule

Bronze: 12-15 stars. Silver: 16-17 stars. Gold: 18-20 stars. Completion requires quiz practice and a speaking attempt.
