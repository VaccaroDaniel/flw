Teacher focus

  • Mission setup: Show the Hello Badge Mission cover and explain today's challenge: say hello, say name, choose greetings.
  • Review quiz: Use the choose-the-greeting image. Children choose Hello/Goodbye and Hi/Bye.
  • Speaking mission: Children say Hello! I'm ___. Teacher or system gives feedback.
  • Badge reward: Show the reward image. Give a sticker, star, or digital badge.
  • Reflection: Children say: I can say hello. I can say my name.
