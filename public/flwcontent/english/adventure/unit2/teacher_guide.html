Teacher Guide

Pre-A1 young learner unit: colors, picture learning, TPR, song, games, and final badge mission.

Lesson 1: Color Discovery

Children can recognize and say five color words: red, blue, yellow, green, purple.

Knowledge Points

  • EN-PREA1-U02-L01-KP001 Recognize five basic color words
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L01-KP002 Say color words with visual support
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L01-KP003 Point to a color when hearing the word

Procedure

StageTeacher action
Warm-upShow the Unit 2 cover. Ask children to name any colors they already know. Use gesture and praise.
InputUse the color chart. Say each word slowly with a clear gesture: red, blue, yellow, green, purple.
Controlled practiceChildren point to the color swatch when the teacher says the word. Repeat in different voices.
Character practiceUse Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto images. Ask: What color is it? Children answer with one word.
Mini-checkChildren choose or point to the correct color card. Accept pointing, touching, or saying the color.

Classroom language

Look at the colors. | Listen and point. | Say red. Say blue. | Great! One more time. | What color is it?

Audio script

  • red, blue, yellow, green, purple
  • What color is it?
  • It's red. It's blue. It's yellow. It's green. It's purple.

Moodle implementation

  • Page: Unit cover and color chart
  • Quiz: Listen and choose the color
  • H5P-style: tap the matching color
  • Completion: view lesson + 70% quiz + speaking attempt

Assessment

Can identify at least 4 of 5 colors and say at least 3 color words clearly.

Lesson 2: Color Worlds

Children can answer “What color is it?” using “It’s + color.”

Knowledge Points

  • EN-PREA1-U02-L02-KP001 Understand color-object association
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L02-KP002 Produce short phrase: It is / It's + color
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L02-KP003 Identify red, blue, yellow, green, purple in picture scenes

Procedure

StageTeacher action
ReviewFlash color cards quickly. Children say the color or hold up matching crayons.
Red/Blue/Yellow/Green/Purple scenesShow each color-world picture. Children point to 3 objects in that color.
Sentence buildingTeacher models: It is red. Then natural form: It’s red.
Pair/Group practiceOne child points, another says the color. Keep it playful and short.
Mini-checkUse picture cards and ask: What color is it? Children answer with one-word or “It’s...” sentence.

Classroom language

What color is it? | It's red. | Point to the blue object. | Find something green. | Good job!

Audio script

  • What color is it?
  • It's red.
  • It's blue.
  • It's yellow.
  • It's green.
  • It's purple.

Moodle implementation

  • Quiz: Choose the correct color word
  • True/False: The apple is red
  • Fill blank: It's ____
  • Completion: 70% and one oral sentence

Assessment

Can answer at least 3 questions with “It’s + color.”

Lesson 3: Find the Colors

Children can follow simple instructions: find, point to, match, and drag.

Knowledge Points

  • EN-PREA1-U02-L03-KP001 Understand action instructions: find / point to / match
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L03-KP002 Match color words to objects
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L03-KP003 Identify color in a complex picture

Procedure

StageTeacher action
Warm-upUse TPR: point, find, touch, match. Children act before speaking.
Look-and-findShow the playroom and garden pictures. Children find target colors.
Target taskUse “Find the red ball” and “Point to the blue bag.” Give plenty of wait time.
MatchingChildren match color labels to objects orally first, then on paper/screen.
Mini-checkGive 5 commands and record how many children can follow independently.

Classroom language

Find the red ball. | Point to the blue bag. | Match the color. | Drag the object. | Try again. You can do it.

Audio script

  • Find the red ball.
  • Point to the blue bag.
  • Match red.
  • Match blue.
  • Drag the object.

Moodle implementation

  • H5P-style: drag object to color
  • Quiz: click correct object
  • Ordering/matching activity
  • Completion: correct 4 of 5 match items

Assessment

Can follow at least 4 of 5 color instructions with visual support.

Lesson 4: Color Song and Games

Children can chant the color words and play short review games.

Knowledge Points

  • EN-PREA1-U02-L04-KP001 Say colors rhythmically in a chant
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L04-KP002 Use color words in a game context
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L04-KP003 Build confidence through repetition and movement

Procedure

StageTeacher action
Song inputPlay or chant slowly. Children listen first, then clap with the rhythm.
TPR songAssign actions: clap red, wave blue, touch head yellow, jump green, spin purple.
Color train gameChildren match colors to train cars or balloons.
Toto quizToto asks: What color is it? Children answer as a group, then individually.
PerformanceClass sings once without teacher support.

Classroom language

Sing with me. | Clap and say red. | Jump for blue. | What color is it? | Let’s play again.

Audio script

  • Color song chorus: Red, blue, yellow, green, purple!
  • What color is it?
  • It's red. It's blue.

Moodle implementation

  • Interactive video/song page
  • Quiz: color train choice
  • Toto game: choose answer
  • Speaking: record 3 colors

Assessment

Can join the chant and answer at least 3 Toto color questions.

Lesson 5: Color Badge Mission

Children complete the final color mission and receive a Color Badge.

Knowledge Points

  • EN-PREA1-U02-L05-KP001 Review and integrate five colors
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L05-KP002 Sort objects by color
  • EN-PREA1-U02-L05-KP003 Complete a simple color self-check

Procedure

StageTeacher action
Mission setupShow the Color Badge Mission cover and explain the 5 gates: listen, say, find, match, sort.
Basket gameChildren sort objects into the correct baskets. Encourage speaking while sorting.
Final oral checkAsk each child 3 color questions. Accept one-word or full sentence answers.
CertificateChildren color/write their favorite color and receive the badge.
ReflectionChildren say: I can say colors!

Classroom language

Are you ready? | Choose the color. | Put it in the basket. | Say the color. | You are a color superstar!

Audio script

  • Welcome to the Color Badge Mission!
  • Put the apple in the red basket.
  • What color is it?
  • You did it!

Moodle implementation

  • Final quiz: 10 questions
  • Drag/sort basket activity
  • Speaking assignment: say 5 colors
  • Completion: score 70% + submit speaking

Assessment

Color Badge: 12/20 stars or higher. Gold Badge: 18/20 stars or higher.
