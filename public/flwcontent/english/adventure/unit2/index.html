Unit Map: My Colors

Move through five short steps: discover colors, use color sentences, find colors, play with color words, and complete the Color Badge Mission.

Pronunciation

Say color words clearly and hear the first sound in short color commands.

/r/ sound

Listen for: redrainbow

Say it: red rainbow

/g/ sound

Listen for: green

Say it: green gate

Color sentence rhythm

Listen for: It's red.Find blue.

Say it: It's red. Find blue.

Practice route: listen once, repeat, mark the strong word, then use it in the lesson task.

Point to a color first, then say the word.
