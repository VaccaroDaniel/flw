# Adventure English World V2 — Unit 6 Full Lesson Content

**Unit:** 6 — Mini Quest Review  
**Band:** Pre-A1.1  
**Can-do:** I can use early words and phrases in a picture mission.  
**Main review:** greetings, colors, numbers, classroom actions, animals.  
**Final Project:** My Mini Picture Mission  

## Unit Story
- Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto see five quest badges.
- Badge 1: Hello. Badge 2: Color. Badge 3: Number. Badge 4: Action. Badge 5: Animal.
- The team says the words and makes a mini picture mission.

**Story model lines:**
- 🔊 Hello. I’m Leo.
- 🔊 It’s blue.
- 🔊 How many? Five.
- 🔊 Point.
- 🔊 It’s a dog. I like dogs.
