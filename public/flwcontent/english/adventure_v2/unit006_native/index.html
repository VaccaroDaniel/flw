Unit 6

Mini Quest Review

I can use early words and phrases in a picture mission.

Learning Path

HelloColorsNumbersActionsAnimalsMini picture mission

Unit Story

Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto see five quest badges.

Badge 1: Hello. Badge 2: Color. Badge 3: Number. Badge 4: Action. Badge 5: Animal.

The team says the words and makes a mini picture mission.

Hello. I’m Leo.
It’s blue.
How many? Five.
It’s a dog. I like dogs.
＋Lesson 1 — Hello Gate

I can say hello and say my name.

Study

Leo waves at the gate. Mia and Emma wave too. Toto sits by the gate.

Use these short lines to start the mini quest.

Listen and say

Hello.
Hi.
I’m Leo.
I’m Mia.
I’m Emma.
Goodbye.

Your turn

Say: Hello. I’m ___. Goodbye.

Practice
