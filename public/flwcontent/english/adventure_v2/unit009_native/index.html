Snack Time

I can name simple foods and ask politely.

Learning path: snack/drink words → I want …, please → a/an snack chunks → short role-play.
StoryLessonsWatchProjectProgress

Snack Time Story

Today I can ask politely for a snack or drink.

  • Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto are at the classroom snack table.
  • Leo sees an apple. Mia sees juice. Emma sees a sandwich.
  • The team uses polite words.
  • Leo says, “I want an apple, please.”
  • Mia says, “I want juice, please.”
  • Emma says, “I want a sandwich, please.”
  • Toto sits and smiles. Snack time is kind and polite.
−Lesson 1: Snack Words

I can point to and say snack words.

Study

Look. Listen. Point.

apple · banana · orange · sandwich · cookie

Extra picture words: cake, bread. These are look-and-say words only.

Model

LeoApple.
MiaBanana.
EmmaCookie.

Your turn

Point to three snacks and say the word: apple, banana, cookie.

Repair: If you are not sure, look at the tray again and say one word only.

−Lesson 2: I Want...

I can say what snack I want.

Study

Listen. Choose. Say.

I want an apple.

I want a banana.

I want a sandwich.

I want a cookie.

Model

LeoI want an apple.
MiaI want a banana.
EmmaI want a cookie.

Your turn

Choose one snack. Say: I want _____.

Repair: Start with the chunk I want. Then add one snack word.

−Lesson 3: Please

I can ask for a snack politely.

Study

Polite snack words:

I want a banana, please.

I want an apple, please.

Thank you.

Model

MiaI want a banana, please.
LeoHere you are.
MiaThank you.

Your turn

Ask a friend politely: I want a/an _____, please.

Repair: If please is missing, say the whole line again with please at the end.

−Lesson 4: A / An Snack Chunks

I can notice and use fixed snack chunks: a banana, an apple.

Study

Listen and copy the chunk. Do not explain it. Just say it.

a banana · a sandwich · a cookie

an apple · an orange

Model

Leoan apple
Miaa banana
Emmaan orange

Your turn

Hold a snack card. Say the full chunk: a banana / an apple.

Repair: Use the repair picture. Copy the full chunk, not only the food word.

−Lesson 5: Drinks

I can name simple drinks and choose one drink.

Study

Drink words:

water · milk · juice

I want water, please.

I want milk, please.

I want juice, please.

Model

LeoI want water, please.
MiaI want juice, please.
EmmaI want milk, please.

Your turn

Choose one drink and say: I want _____, please.

Repair: For drinks, use the drink word after I want: I want juice, please.

−Lesson 6: Snack Order Role-play

I can ask for one snack and one drink in a short role-play.

Study

Role-play model:

Helper: Hello.

Learner: I want an apple and water, please.

Helper: Here you are.

Learner: Thank you.

Model

HelperHello.
LearnerI want a sandwich and juice, please.
HelperHere you are.
LearnerThank you.

Your turn

Work with a partner. Choose one snack and one drink. Say the role-play.

Repair: If the line is too long, say it in two parts: I want a sandwich. I want juice, please.

−Lesson 7: Project Rehearsal

I can make and rehearse my snack order card.

Study

Project model card:

My snack: an apple.

My drink: water.

My line: I want an apple and water, please.

Model

EmmaMy snack is a cookie. My drink is juice.
EmmaI want a cookie and juice, please.

Your turn

Draw or choose one snack and one drink. Practice your line two times.

Repair: Check three parts: snack word, drink word, please.

Project connection: Learners reuse the same pattern in My Snack Order Role-play.

−Final Project: My Snack Order Role-play

Make one snack order card and say one short role-play.

Useful phrases

  • Hello.
  • I want an apple, please.
  • I want a banana and juice, please.
  • Here you are.
  • Thank you.

Project steps

  1. Choose one snack: an apple, a banana, an orange, a sandwich, or a cookie.
  2. Choose one drink: water, milk, or juice.
  3. Make a simple snack order card.
  4. Practice the line: I want ____ and ____, please.
  5. Say the role-play with a helper: Hello. / I want ____ and ____, please. / Here you are. / Thank you.

Portfolio evidence: photo of snack order card + short speaking recording of the role-play

What I can do now

  • I can name simple snacks and drinks.
  • I can say: I want …, please.
  • I can use fixed chunks like a banana and an apple.
  • I can do a short snack order role-play.
