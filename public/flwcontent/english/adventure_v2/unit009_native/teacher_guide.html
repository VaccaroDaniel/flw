Teacher Guide — Adventure English World V2 Unit 9: Snack Time

Level: Pre-A1.2 · Can-do: I can name simple foods and ask politely.

Unit overview

This unit teaches simple snack and drink words, the polite chunk I want …, please, and fixed a/an snack chunks only. The final project is My Snack Order Role-play.

Blocked content: restaurant/café menu language, prices/money, would like, Can I have …?, some/any, health advice, allergies, cooking, and paragraph writing.

Teaching sequence

Lesson 1: Snack Words

Can-do: I can point to and say snack words.

KP: VOC: snack words; LST: listen and point; SP: repeat single words

Focus words: apple, banana, orange, sandwich, cookie, cake, bread

Teacher note: Keep production oral and supported. Use the picture before asking learners to choose or speak.

Repair: If you are not sure, look at the tray again and say one word only.

Lesson 2: I Want...

Can-do: I can say what snack I want.

KP: CHK: I want ...; VOC: snack choice; SI: answer a simple choice question

Focus words: I want, apple, banana, orange, sandwich, cookie

Teacher note: Keep production oral and supported. Use the picture before asking learners to choose or speak.

Repair: Start with the chunk I want. Then add one snack word.

Lesson 3: Please

Can-do: I can ask for a snack politely.

KP: CHK: I want ..., please.; REG: friendly polite asking; SI: helper/learner exchange

Focus words: please, thank you, I want

Teacher note: Keep production oral and supported. Use the picture before asking learners to choose or speak.

Repair: If please is missing, say the whole line again with please at the end.

Lesson 4: A / An Snack Chunks

Can-do: I can notice and use fixed snack chunks: a banana, an apple.

KP: GRM: a/an noticing as fixed chunks only; VOC: snack chunks; STR: repair by model copying

Focus words: a banana, a sandwich, a cookie, an apple, an orange

Teacher note: Keep production oral and supported. Use the picture before asking learners to choose or speak.

Repair: Use the repair picture. Copy the full chunk, not only the food word.

Lesson 5: Drinks

Can-do: I can name simple drinks and choose one drink.

KP: VOC: drink words; CHK: I want water/milk/juice, please.; SI: simple choice

Focus words: water, milk, juice, drink

Teacher note: Keep production oral and supported. Use the picture before asking learners to choose or speak.

Repair: For drinks, use the drink word after I want: I want juice, please.

Lesson 6: Snack Order Role-play

Can-do: I can ask for one snack and one drink in a short role-play.

KP: SI: snack order role-play; CHK: I want ..., please.; VOC: snack + drink; REG: thank you

Focus words: hello, I want, please, here you are, thank you, snack, drink

Teacher note: Keep production oral and supported. Use the picture before asking learners to choose or speak.

Repair: If the line is too long, say it in two parts: I want a sandwich. I want juice, please.

Lesson 7: Project Rehearsal

Can-do: I can make and rehearse my snack order card.

KP: PRJ: project rehearsal; SP: short snack order; WRT: copy one snack word and one drink word; STR: self-check

Focus words: snack card, apple, banana, sandwich, water, milk, juice, please

Teacher note: Keep production oral and supported. Use the picture before asking learners to choose or speak.

Repair: Check three parts: snack word, drink word, please.

Watch teaching notes

Title: Toto and the Snack Card

Length: about 54 seconds. Use it as a silent/captioned comprehension and repetition support.

  1. Snack Time. Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto are ready.
  2. Leo looks at the snacks.
  3. Leo says, “I want an apple, please.”
  4. Mia looks at the drinks.
  5. Mia says, “I want juice, please.”
  6. Emma looks at the snack card.
  7. Emma says, “I want a sandwich, please.”
  8. Toto sits by the table.
  9. The team says, “Thank you!” Snack time is polite.

Project rubric

  • Names one snack and one drink.
  • Uses I want …, please.
  • Uses polite closing Thank you.
  • Shows a simple snack order card.
  • Speaks clearly enough for a partner to understand.

Repair path

  • Vocabulary: return to snack/drink picture cards.
  • Polite request: copy and repeat I want …, please.
  • a/an chunks: practice fixed chunks only: a banana, an apple, an orange.
  • Role-play order: Hello → order → Here you are → Thank you.
