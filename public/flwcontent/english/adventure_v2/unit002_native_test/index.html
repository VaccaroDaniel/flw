Lesson 1 — Hello, Color Quest!

I can join the quest and say a color word.Focus: hello review + first colors

Listen and point

Hello, Leo. Hello, Mia. Hello, Emma. Hello, Toto. Color Quest! red blue yellow green

Say

What color? red What color? blue Goodbye.

Tiny chant

Red, blue. Yellow, green. Color Quest! Goodbye!
Lesson 2 — Meet the Colors

I can match color words to color cards.Focus: 10 color words

Listen and repeat

red blue yellow green orange purple pink black white brown

Point and say

red blue yellow green orange purple pink black white brown

Mini pattern

What color? red What color? orange
Lesson 3 — It’s Red!

I can say It’s + color.Focus: It’s + color

Listen

It’s red. It’s blue. It’s yellow. It’s green.

Dialogue

Leo: It’s red. Mia: It’s blue. Emma: It’s yellow.

Say

It’s red. It’s blue. It’s yellow. It’s green.

Color cards

It’s orange. It’s purple.
Lesson 4 — Color Objects

I can choose the color of a classroom object.Focus: color + simple classroom object support

Listen and point

red book blue bag yellow pencil green box

Say the color

What color? It’s red. What color? It’s blue. What color? It’s yellow. What color? It’s green.

More color cards

orange box purple book pink bag black chair white box brown box
Lesson 5 — Color Clue Hunt

I can listen and choose a color clue.Focus: listening for color clues

Story

Hello! Color Quest! Leo: It’s red. Mia: It’s blue. Emma: It’s yellow. Toto: It’s green. Goodbye!

Listen and follow

red blue yellow green

Say

It’s red. It’s blue. It’s yellow. It’s green.
Lesson 6 — Toto’s Color Story

I can review colors in a tiny story.Focus: review all colors + Toto finds brown, orange, pink

Lesson 7 — My Color Mini-Poster

I can make and say my color poster.Focus: project rehearsal

Project model

I’m ___. red blue green

Say your poster

Hello. I’m ___. It’s red. It’s blue. Goodbye.

Optional color line

It’s green.
