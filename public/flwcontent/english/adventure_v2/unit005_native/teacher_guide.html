Teacher Guide — Adventure English World V2 Unit 5: Animal Friends

Level: Pre-A1.1. Can-do: I can name animals and say what I like. Final project: My Animal Choice Card.

Unit Overview

This unit teaches animal words and the chunks “It’s a...,” “I like...,” and “I don’t like...” through simple picture-supported interaction. The unit is intentionally controlled for Pre-A1.1 learners.

Allowed KP

  • animal words: dog, cat, bird, fish, rabbit, duck, frog, turtle
  • chunks: It’s a..., I like..., I don’t like...
  • very short listening and pointing tasks

Blocked KP

Do not add age, phone numbers, dates/time, countries/maps/geography, flags, long self-introductions, animal abilities, has got, because, or grammar tables.

Lesson-by-Lesson Notes

LessonTeaching note
1Introduce dog/cat/bird/fish with pointing and short repetition. Keep output to single words and “It’s a...” sentences.
2Add rabbit/duck/frog/turtle. Use picture matching before speaking.
3Stabilize “It’s a ...” with animal words. Do not introduce grammar tables.
4Teach “I like ...” as a memorized chunk. Model friendly choices.
5Teach “I don’t like ...” gently. Avoid negative animal descriptions.
6Use Toto’s trail for sequence comprehension. Keep sequence to animal names only.
7Prepare final animal choice card and short oral output.

Watch Plan

Use Option A when making the MP4: build a 52-second silent captioned slideshow from approved Unit 5 images. Do not generate separate video-only frames. Use the watch script in assets/video/watch_script.txt.

Repair Path

  • Vocabulary confusion: return to picture cards and matching.
  • Missing “a” in “It’s a...”: model and repeat three sentences.
  • Singular/plural in “I like...”: use phrase cards: I like dogs / I like cats / I like birds.
  • Negative sentence confusion: use dog/frog contrast and repeat “I don’t like frogs.”
