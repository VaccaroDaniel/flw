Adventure English World V2Unit 5Pre-A1.1

Animal Friends

I can name animals and say what I like.
Project: My Animal Choice Card

Main words: dog, cat, bird, fish, rabbit, duck, frog, turtle. Main sentences: It’s a..., I like..., I don’t like...

Unit Story

Hello, Leo. Hello, Mia. Hello, Emma. Hello, Toto!

Look! A dog. Look! A cat. Look! A bird. Look! A fish.

Toto sees a rabbit. Toto sees a duck. Toto sees a frog. Toto sees a turtle.

Leo says, “I like dogs.” Mia says, “I like cats.” Emma says, “I like birds.”

Animal friends! Choose your animal.

Lesson 1 — Hello, Animal Friends

Can-do: I can name four animals: dog, cat, bird, fish.

Study
dog
cat
bird
fish
Model dialogue 🔊

It’s a dog. It’s a cat. It’s a bird. It’s a fish.

Your turn

Point. Say the animal word.

Lesson 2 — More Animal Friends

Can-do: I can name four more animals: rabbit, duck, frog, turtle.

Study
rabbit
duck
frog
turtle
Model dialogue 🔊

It’s a rabbit. It’s a duck. It’s a frog. It’s a turtle.

Your turn

Point to an animal. Say the word.

Lesson 3 — It’s a ...

Can-do: I can say “It’s a ...” with animal words.

Study

It’s a + animal.

It’s a dog. It’s a cat. It’s a bird. It’s a fish.

It’s a rabbit. It’s a duck. It’s a frog. It’s a turtle.

Your turn

Choose an animal card. Say: It’s a ___.

Lesson 4 — I Like Animals

Can-do: I can say what animals I like.

Study

I like + animal word.

I like dogs. I like cats. I like birds. I like fish.

I like rabbits. I like ducks. I like frogs. I like turtles.

Model dialogue 🔊

Leo: I like dogs.
Mia: I like cats.
Emma: I like birds.

Your turn

Choose one animal. Say: I like ___.

Lesson 5 — I Don’t Like ...

Can-do: I can say a gentle animal preference.

Study

Sometimes we like one animal. Sometimes we do not like one animal.

I like dogs.
I don’t like frogs.

All animals are friendly. This is only a choice.

Model dialogue 🔊

Leo: I like dogs.
Leo: I don’t like frogs.

Your turn

Choose one: I like ___. / I don’t like ___.

Lesson 6 — Toto’s Animal Trail

Can-do: I can understand a simple animal trail story.

Story

Toto runs. Toto sees a dog. Toto sees a cat. Toto sees a rabbit. Toto sees a duck. Toto likes animal friends.

Listen and Point 🔊

dog → cat → rabbit → duck

Your turn

Say the animal trail: dog → cat → rabbit → duck.

Lesson 7 — My Animal Choice Card

Can-do: I can choose an animal and say two simple sentences.

Study

Choose one animal: dog, cat, bird, fish, rabbit, duck, frog, turtle.

Project Model 🔊

My animal: dog.
It’s a dog.
I like dogs.

My animal: cat.
It’s a cat.
I like cats.

Your turn

Choose an animal. Make a card. Say: It’s a ___. I like ___.

Final Project — My Animal Choice Card

What you make

Make an animal choice card. Choose one animal: dog, cat, bird, fish, rabbit, duck, frog, turtle.

Useful phrases
My animal: dog.
It’s a dog.
I like dogs.
Student output

Show your card and say: It’s a ___. I like ___.
