Unit 8 — Body Move Quest

Can-do: I can name body parts and follow movement instructions.

Level: Pre-A1.2Project: My Movement ChantPractice: 108 items

Unit Story

Toto finds paw-print clues in the adventure classroom. Leo, Mia, and Emma follow the clues. They touch, point, clap, and move. At the end, they make a movement chant.

Words: body and face parts
Commands: Touch your … / Point to your … / Clap your …
Project: say and move in a 4-line chant
