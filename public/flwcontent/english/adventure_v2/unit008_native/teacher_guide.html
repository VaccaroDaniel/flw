Teaching Notes

  • Keep all language oral, visual, and action-based.
  • Model commands first, then ask learners to repeat and move.
  • Use IMG20 for gentle repair: look, listen again, try again.
  • Do not expand into health problems or should/shouldn’t.
  • Final project evidence is a short performed movement chant.

Project Rubric

CriterionReadyNeeds Repair
Body wordsUses 4 body/action words accurately.Confuses body words.
CommandsUses Touch your / Clap your / Point to your.Needs command model support.
MovementPerforms safe matching movements.Needs teacher modeling.
ConfidenceSays chant clearly with support.Repeats after teacher or peer.

Repair Path

Vocabulary confusion → picture-word review. Command confusion → teacher models and learner repeats. My/your confusion → own body vs friend/listener contrast. Listening weakness → replay Watch with captions, then retry three commands.
