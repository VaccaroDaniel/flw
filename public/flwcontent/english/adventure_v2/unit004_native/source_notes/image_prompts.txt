Global style lock for all images:
Semi-realistic modern children/teen Adventure English coursebook style, warm and polished, not too cartoon-like, bright but not childish, clean classroom-adventure atmosphere. Keep the recurring characters consistent with the reference identity card: Leo is an Asian boy with black hair, round glasses, green top, neat school-adventure look; Mia has long brown hair and a yellow cardigan; Emma has blonde hair, purple outfit, purple headband; Toto is a small brown-and-white playful dog with a small name tag. Characters are about 7-8 years old for Pre-A1.1. Use soft warm lighting, rounded coursebook composition, functional learning visuals, readable gestures, and clear action poses.

Global negative prompt for all images:
No jeans, no denim, no short skirts, no striped skirts, no loose trousers, no revealing clothing, no political symbols, no religious promotion, no slogans, no central flags, no violence, no adult content, no distracting logos, no geography map content, no country references, no messy classroom clutter, no cropped faces, no distorted hands, no unreadable random text, no extra recurring characters replacing Leo/Mia/Emma/Toto.

Text-in-image rule:
Avoid readable embedded text in generated images unless specifically requested. Leave clean blank label spaces for HTML overlays. All important words must be repeated in HTML.
