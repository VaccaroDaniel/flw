# Adventure English World V2 - Unit 4 Full Unit Map

## Unit Identity

Unit title: Classroom Magic  
Level: Pre-A1.1  
Learner age identity: Adventure World early child learners, about 7-8 years old  
Main situation: Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto enter a friendly adventure classroom where classroom commands become a simple "magic action" game.  
Unit mission: Listen, understand, and act out simple classroom instructions.  
Final portfolio output: Classroom Action Recording - the learner chooses four action cards, says each command, performs the action, and records a short video or audio.

## Character And Visual Lock

Use the Adventure World character identity card as the reference:

- Leo: Asian boy, black hair, round glasses, green top, neat school-adventure look.
- Mia: girl with long brown hair, yellow cardigan, warm cheerful expression.
- Emma: blonde girl, purple outfit, purple headband, friendly expression.
- Toto: small brown-and-white dog, playful, cute, with a small name tag.

Unit style: semi-realistic modern Adventure English coursebook, warm classroom-adventure mood, not too cartoon-like. No jeans, no denim, no short skirts, no striped skirts, no loose trousers, no distracting logos, no flags, no political or religious symbols.

## Learning Progression

The unit is built as a movement from recognition to response to production:

1. Hear the classroom magic commands.
2. Match command to action picture.
3. Follow commands physically.
4. Say short commands with "please".
5. Combine commands into a mini classroom routine.
6. Watch the team follow commands.
7. Record the learner's own classroom action sequence.

## Difficulty Guarantee

Unit 4 is locked as a Pre-A1.1 listen-and-do unit. Its difficulty comes from action recognition, short listening, simple sequencing, and confident response, not from grammar explanation or longer speaking. The external young-learner benchmark for this lock is saved in `source_notes/young_learner_difficulty_benchmark.md`.

Coursebook-style principle: picture first, whole-listening model second, short Study First chunk third, quiz/action output fourth. Every lesson must keep the same route so young learners know where to begin.

## Allowed, Review, And Blocked KP

Allowed new KP:

- Imperatives as memorized classroom chunks: Listen. Look. Point. Say. Stand up. Sit down. Open your book. Close your book. Read. Write.
- Polite classroom chunk: please.
- Action response: learner follows a command without needing full grammar explanation.
- Command recognition through image/audio.
- Command production in a short action-recording project.

Review KP from Units 1-3:

- Greetings: Hello. Hi. Goodbye.
- Names: I'm Leo. I'm Mia. I'm Emma.
- Color recognition as optional classroom-card support.
- Numbers 1-5 as optional sequence support only.
- Character names: Leo, Mia, Emma, Toto.

Blocked KP:

- Animals and likes/dislikes from Unit 5.
- Family, age, countries, nationalities, geography, maps.
- Full imperative grammar explanation.
- Long self-introduction.
- Plural grammar explanations.
- Prepositions and classroom-location grammar.
- Complex modal forms such as Can you...? / You must...

## Skill Balance

Listening: identify and follow teacher commands.  
Speaking: say action commands with clear stress.  
Reading: recognize command cards with icons.  
Writing: copy 3-4 command labels on a classroom action card.  
Mediation: connect a spoken command, an icon, and a classroom action.  
Pronunciation: clear initial sounds in listen, look, point, stand, sit, open, close.  
Portfolio: action recording and action card.

## Lesson Map

### Lesson 1 - The Magic Classroom

Can-do goal: I can hear three classroom commands and choose the right action.  
New language: Listen. Look. Say.  
Input: short teacher routine with Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto.  
Study focus: command = action. No grammar explanation.  
Images: IMG001 cover, IMG002 opener, IMG003 first command board, IMG004 first classroom action scene.  
Practice target: 12 items using image choice, audio choice, command-to-icon matching, and simple sequence.  
Output: learner points to the correct action card.  
Repair path: replay the three-command board, then retry action matching.

### Lesson 2 - Listen, Look, Point

Can-do goal: I can follow listen, look, and point commands.  
New language: Listen. Look. Point.  
Review: Hello; Leo, Mia, Emma, Toto.  
Input: teacher gives three commands while the team follows.  
Study focus: differentiate listening action, looking action, and pointing action.  
Images: IMG005, IMG006, IMG007, IMG008.  
Practice target: 12 items using audio choice, image choice, matching, and action order.  
Output: learner follows three commands in order.  
Repair path: icon board with "ear, eyes, finger" action cues.

### Lesson 3 - Say It Again

Can-do goal: I can repeat a classroom word or phrase.  
New language: Say. Say it again. One more time.  
Review: Hello. Hi. Goodbye.  
Input: Mia says a word, Emma repeats, Leo says it again.  
Study focus: repeat language for classroom practice.  
Images: IMG009, IMG010.  
Practice target: 12 items using command meaning, dialogue order, repeat phrase recognition, and response choice.  
Output: learner says one word twice after a model.  
Repair path: repeat-after-me mini drill.

### Lesson 4 - Stand Up, Sit Down

Can-do goal: I can understand and perform stand up and sit down.  
New language: Stand up. Sit down.  
Review: numbers 1-3 as sequence support only.  
Input: action game with Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto.  
Study focus: body action commands.  
Images: IMG011, IMG012, IMG013.  
Practice target: 12 items using action matching, sequence, true/false image check, and audio choice.  
Output: learner performs stand up and sit down in order.  
Repair path: two-card contrast board.

### Lesson 5 - Open And Close

Can-do goal: I can follow open and close commands with a book.  
New language: Open your book. Close your book.  
Receptive support: card, bag, box, door as image-only classroom objects.  
Input: Leo opens a book; Mia closes a book; Toto watches.  
Study focus: action + classroom object as a memorized phrase.  
Images: IMG014, IMG015, IMG016.  
Practice target: 12 items using object-action matching, audio choice, phrase completion, and action order.  
Output: learner opens and closes a book/card.  
Repair path: open/close contrast visual.

### Lesson 6 - Read And Write

Can-do goal: I can understand read and write in classroom work.  
New language: Read. Write.  
Review: command card recognition from Lessons 1-5.  
Input: Emma reads a card; Leo writes a command label; Mia checks the action card.  
Study focus: read/write as classroom actions, not academic explanation.  
Images: IMG017, IMG018, IMG019.  
Practice target: 12 items using read/write contrast, icon matching, command-card completion, and mini routine sequence.  
Output: learner copies two command labels.  
Repair path: read/write icon board and copy support.

### Lesson 7 - Classroom Magic Mission

Can-do goal: I can choose and say four classroom commands.  
New language: combine commands in a short routine.  
Review: all Unit 4 commands.  
Input: team mission board with six action cards.  
Study focus: select, order, say, and act.  
Images: IMG020, IMG022.  
Practice target: 12 items using command sequence, best command, project readiness, and repair choice.  
Output: learner rehearses final classroom action recording.  
Repair path: choose four safe commands, listen to model, record again.

## Final Project

Project title: My Classroom Action Recording  
Learner outcome:

1. Make a four-card classroom action strip.
2. Choose four commands from Unit 4.
3. Say each command with "please".
4. Act out or point to each command.
5. Submit recording and card image.

Required language:

- Hello. I'm ___.
- [Command], please.
- Goodbye.

Recommended command choices:

- Listen, please.
- Look, please.
- Point, please.
- Stand up, please.
- Sit down, please.
- Open your book, please.
- Close your book, please.
- Read, please.
- Write, please.

AI/speaking checker focus:

- Task completion: four commands included.
- Pronunciation/intelligibility: command words understandable.
- Fluency: commands spoken without long silence.
- Language control: only Unit 4 allowed language plus Unit 1 greeting/name.
- Action match: spoken command matches action card or gesture.

Portfolio evidence:

- classroom action card image
- voice/video action recording
- practice completion record
- AI/teacher feedback note

## Quality Gate Notes
