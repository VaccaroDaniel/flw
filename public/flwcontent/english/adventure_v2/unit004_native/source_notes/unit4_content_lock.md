# Adventure English World V2 - Unit 4 Content Lock

Unit title: Classroom Magic  
Level: Pre-A1.1  
Final output: My Classroom Action Recording

## Unit Story

Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto enter a bright classroom where action cards glow like "magic." The magic is simple: when learners hear a command, they understand it, choose the right picture, and do the action.

The unit must feel like a classroom game, not a grammar explanation.

## Lesson Content Outline

### Lesson 1 - The Magic Classroom

Language: Listen. Look. Say.  
Model:

- Teacher: Listen, please.
- Teacher: Look, please.
- Teacher: Say "hello."
- Children: Hello!

Study First:

- Listen = use your ears.
- Look = use your eyes.
- Say = use your voice.

Output: choose the correct action card.

### Lesson 2 - Listen, Look, Point

Language: Listen. Look. Point.  
Model:

- Teacher: Listen, please.
- Teacher: Look, please.
- Teacher: Point, please.

Study First:

- listen -> ear
- look -> eyes
- point -> finger

Output: follow three commands in order.

### Lesson 3 - Say It Again

Language: Say. Say it again. One more time.  
Model:

- Mia: Hello.
- Emma: Hello.
- Leo: Say it again.
- Mia and Emma: Hello!

Study First:

- Say.
- Say it again.
- One more time.

Output: repeat one command or greeting after a model.

### Lesson 4 - Stand Up, Sit Down

Language: Stand up. Sit down.  
Model:

- Teacher: Stand up, please.
- Children stand.
- Teacher: Sit down, please.
- Children sit.

Study First:

- Stand up.
- Sit down.
- Stop.

Output: act out stand up and sit down in order.

### Lesson 5 - Open And Close

Language: Open your book. Close your book.  
Model:

- Teacher: Open your book, please.
- Leo opens his book.
- Teacher: Close your book, please.
- Emma closes her book.

Study First:

- Open your book.
- Close your book.
- please

Output: open and close a real book/card.

### Lesson 6 - Read And Write

Language: Read. Write.  
Model:

- Teacher: Read, please.
- Emma reads a card.
- Teacher: Write, please.
- Leo writes a word card.

Study First:

- Read.
- Write.
- Read and write.

Output: copy two command labels.

### Lesson 7 - Classroom Magic Mission

Language: review all Unit 4 commands.  
Model:

- Leo: Listen, please.
- Mia: Stand up, please.
- Emma: Open your book, please.
- All: Classroom magic!

Output: choose four commands and rehearse the final recording.

### Final Project - My Classroom Action Recording

Required learner script:

- Hello. I'm ___.
- [Command], please.
- [Command], please.
- [Command], please.
- [Command], please.
- Goodbye.

Checker criteria:

- four commands included
- commands are from Unit 4
- "please" is used
- action matches command
- pronunciation is understandable
