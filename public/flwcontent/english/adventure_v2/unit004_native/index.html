Unit Brief

Mission: follow classroom commands

Young learners move from recognition to action to short spoken production. The language is intentionally narrow and Pre-A1: commands, please, and a short greeting review.

Listen / Look / Point

Study First

Listen.

Ear action.

Look.

Eye action.

Point.

Finger action.

Point, please.

Polite command.

Notice: Listen is not look. Look is not point. Each command has a different body action.

Whole Listening Model

Whole model audio
  1. Teacher: Listen, please.
  2. Teacher: Look, please.
  3. Teacher: Point, please.

3Lesson 3 - Say It AgainI can repeat a classroom word or short phrase.

Say / Say it again / One more time

Study First

Say.

Use your voice.

Say it again.

Repeat the word.

One more time.

Repeat once more.

Great!

Good classroom response.

Notice: Repeat language helps you practise. You do not need a long sentence.

Whole Listening Model

Whole model audio
  1. Mia: Hello.
  2. Emma: Hello.
  3. Leo: Say it again.
  4. Mia and Emma: Hello!
Output: Say one classroom word twice after the model.
Repair path: Listen, pause, and repeat one short phrase.
4Lesson 4 - Stand Up, Sit DownI can understand and perform stand up and sit down.

Stand up / Sit down / Stop

Study First

Stand up.

Move from chair to standing.

Sit down.

Sit on the chair.

Stop.

Freeze the action.

Stand up, please.

Polite action command.

Notice: Use the whole action. Do not only say the word.

Whole Listening Model

Whole model audio
  1. Teacher: Stand up, please.
  2. Children stand.
  3. Teacher: Sit down, please.
  4. Children sit.
Output: Act out stand up and sit down in order.
Repair path: Compare the stand card and sit card.
