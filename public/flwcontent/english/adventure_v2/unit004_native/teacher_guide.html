Teacher Guide

Unit 4
Classroom Magic

Pre-A1.1 listen-and-do command unit. The difficulty is protected by a short command set, whole-listening models, action response, repair paths, and controlled final recording.

Unit Goals

Learners understand, choose, say, and perform classroom commands: Listen, Look, Point, Say, Say it again, Stand up, Sit down, Open your book, Close your book, Read, Write, Stop, and please.

Final portfolio: My Classroom Action Recording - four command cards plus short voice/video evidence.

Teaching Notes

  1. Start with the picture. Ask learners to point before reading any command.
  2. Play the whole model once, then practise individual chunks.
  3. Keep grammar implicit: command equals action.
  4. When a learner is wrong, use the repair board and retry before showing the answer.
  5. For the project, score four things: command included, please included, action match, clear pronunciation.
