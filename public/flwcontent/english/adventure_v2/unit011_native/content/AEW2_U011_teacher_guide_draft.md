# Teacher Guide Draft — Adventure English World V2 Unit 11: Weather Window

## Overview
Unit 11 teaches Pre-A1.2 learners to recognize simple weather, say **It's ...**, name basic clothes, and choose clothes for weather.

## Core KP
- VOC: sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy
- VOC: coat, hat, boots, T-shirt, shoes, umbrella, jacket, sweater
- CHK: It's sunny/rainy/windy/cloudy.
- MED: choose clothes for a friend
- PRJ: My Weather Choice Card

## Lesson Notes
### Lesson 1 — Meet the Weather
- Goal: I can point to and say weather words.
- Images: AEW2_U011_IMG03, AEW2_U011_IMG04
- Repair: Point to one weather picture. Say the weather word.

### Lesson 2 — It's Sunny / It's Rainy
- Goal: I can say It's sunny and It's rainy.
- Images: AEW2_U011_IMG05, AEW2_U011_IMG06
- Repair: Choose one window picture. Say: It's sunny. or It's rainy.

### Lesson 3 — It's Windy / It's Cloudy
- Goal: I can say It's windy and It's cloudy.
- Images: AEW2_U011_IMG07, AEW2_U011_IMG08
- Repair: Point to the window. Say: It's windy. or It's cloudy.

### Lesson 4 — Clothes Basics
- Goal: I can name simple clothes.
- Images: AEW2_U011_IMG09, AEW2_U011_IMG10
- Repair: Choose one clothes picture. Say the clothes word.

### Lesson 5 — Choose Clothes
- Goal: I can choose clothes for the weather.
- Images: AEW2_U011_IMG11, AEW2_U011_IMG12
- Repair: Look at one weather picture. Choose clothes for it.

### Lesson 6 — Help a Friend
- Goal: I can help a friend choose clothes.
- Images: AEW2_U011_IMG13, AEW2_U011_IMG14, AEW2_U011_IMG15
- Repair: Help Mia, Leo, or Emma. Say one short choice.

### Lesson 7 — Project Rehearsal
- Goal: I can make a simple weather choice card.
- Images: AEW2_U011_IMG16, AEW2_U011_IMG17
- Repair: Make your card. Say the weather and one clothes choice.


## Watch Guidance
Play or show the Watch video once for gist, then again for detail. Learner-facing Watch lines should not show time bands.

## Project Rubric
- Weather picture is clear.
- Clothes choice matches weather.
- Learner says one weather sentence or chunk.
- Learner uses a short choice chunk.

## Remediation
- Weather confusion → use IMG18 and repeat sunny/rainy/windy/cloudy.
- Clothes confusion → use IMG19 and point/say clothes words.
- Chunk confusion → model **It's ...** and **Choose ...** separately.
