# Adventure English World V2 — Unit 11: Weather Window

## Unit Identity
- **Level:** Pre-A1.2
- **Can-do:** I can say simple weather words and choose clothes.
- **KP:** weather words; clothes basics; **It's ...**; simple choosing for a friend
- **Final Project:** My Weather Choice Card
- **Practice target:** 108 on-page Practice items

## Unit Story
Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto look through the Weather Window. One day is sunny. One day is rainy. One day is windy. One day is cloudy. The team chooses simple clothes for each day. They help a friend and make a Weather Choice Card.

## Learning Path
1. Meet the Weather
2. It's Sunny / It's Rainy
3. It's Windy / It's Cloudy
4. Clothes Basics
5. Choose Clothes
6. Help a Friend
7. Project Rehearsal
8. Watch: The Weather Window
9. Final Project: My Weather Choice Card


## Lesson 1 — Meet the Weather
**Can-do:** I can point to and say weather words.

**Images:** AEW2_U011_IMG03, AEW2_U011_IMG04

### Study
Look at the weather window. Point to the weather. Say one word: sunny, rainy, windy, or cloudy.

### Model
- sunny
- rainy
- windy
- cloudy


### Your turn
Point to one weather picture. Say the weather word.

### Words / Chunks
sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy

### Practice
Use Practice items AEW2-U011-L1-Q01–Q12.

## Lesson 2 — It's Sunny / It's Rainy
**Can-do:** I can say It's sunny and It's rainy.

**Images:** AEW2_U011_IMG05, AEW2_U011_IMG06

### Study

### Model

- It's rainy.

### Words / Chunks

## Lesson 7 — Project Rehearsal
**Can-do:** I can make a simple weather choice card.

**Images:** AEW2_U011_IMG16, AEW2_U011_IMG17

### Study
A weather choice card has one weather picture and matching clothes pictures.

### Model
- It's rainy. Choose boots. Choose an umbrella.
- It's sunny. Choose a hat.


### Your turn
Make your card. Say the weather and one clothes choice.

### Words / Chunks
weather card, sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy, choose, coat, hat, boots, umbrella

### Practice
Use Practice items AEW2-U011-L7-Q01–Q12.

## Watch — The Weather Window
**Watch line display rule:** learner-facing Watch lines do not show time bands. Timing belongs only in the VTT when video is produced.

- Leo opens the Weather Window.
- Mia sees the sun. It's sunny.
- Emma sees rain. It's rainy.
- Toto watches leaves move. It's windy.
- The team sees big clouds. It's cloudy.
- Leo chooses a hat for a sunny day.
- Mia chooses boots and an umbrella for a rainy day.
- Emma chooses a sweater for a cloudy day.
- The team helps a friend choose clothes.
- Now it is your turn: make a Weather Choice Card.

Use Practice items AEW2-U011-W-Q01–Q12.

## Final Project — My Weather Choice Card
Make a simple card with:
- one weather picture
- one or two matching clothes pictures
- one short spoken model: **It's rainy. Choose boots.** or **It's sunny. Choose a hat.**

Use Practice items AEW2-U011-P-Q01–Q12.
