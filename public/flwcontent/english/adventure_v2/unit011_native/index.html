Adventure English World V2 — Unit 11: Weather Window

Level: Pre-A1.2 Goal: I can say simple weather words and choose clothes. Project: My Weather Choice Card

Learning Path

Unit Story

Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto look through the Weather Window. One day is sunny. One day is rainy. One day is windy. One day is cloudy. The team chooses simple clothes for each day. They help a friend and make a Weather Choice Card.

Lesson 1 Meet the Weather

I can: I can point to and say weather words.

Study

Look at the weather window. Point to the weather. Say one word: sunny, rainy, windy, or cloudy.

Model

  • sunny
  • rainy
  • windy
  • cloudy

Your turn

Point to one weather picture. Say the weather word.

Words: sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time
Lesson 2 It's Sunny / It's Rainy

I can: I can say It's sunny and It's rainy.

Study

Look outside. Use the chunk It's ... with sunny or rainy.

Model

  • It's sunny.
  • It's rainy.

Your turn

Choose one window picture. Say: It's sunny. or It's rainy.

Words: It's sunny., It's rainy., sunny, rainy

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time
Lesson 3 It's Windy / It's Cloudy

I can: I can say It's windy and It's cloudy.

Study

Look for moving leaves for windy. Look for many clouds for cloudy.

Model

  • It's windy.
  • It's cloudy.

Your turn

Point to the window. Say: It's windy. or It's cloudy.

Words: It's windy., It's cloudy., windy, cloudy

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time
Lesson 4 Clothes Basics

I can: I can name simple clothes.

Study

Look at the clothes. Point and say one clothes word.

Model

  • coat
  • hat
  • boots
  • T-shirt
  • shoes
  • umbrella
  • jacket
  • sweater

Your turn

Choose one clothes picture. Say the clothes word.

Words: coat, hat, boots, T-shirt, shoes, umbrella, jacket, sweater

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time
Lesson 5 Choose Clothes

I can: I can choose clothes for the weather.

Study

Look at the weather. Choose one or two clothes items.

Model

  • Sunny day: choose a hat.
  • Rainy day: choose boots.
  • Rainy day: choose an umbrella.

Your turn

Look at one weather picture. Choose clothes for it.

Words: choose, coat, boots, hat, T-shirt, umbrella, sunny, rainy

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time
Lesson 6 Help a Friend

I can: I can help a friend choose clothes.

Study

A friend looks at the weather. Help with one short choice.

Model

  • It's rainy. Choose boots.
  • It's sunny. Choose a hat.
  • It's windy. Choose a jacket.

Your turn

Help Mia, Leo, or Emma. Say one short choice.

Words: friend, choose, coat, boots, hat, umbrella, shoes

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time
Lesson 7 Project Rehearsal

I can: I can make a simple weather choice card.

Study

A weather choice card has one weather picture and matching clothes pictures.

Model

  • It's rainy. Choose boots. Choose an umbrella.
  • It's sunny. Choose a hat.

Your turn

Make your card. Say the weather and one clothes choice.

Words: weather card, sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy, choose, coat, hat, boots, umbrella

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time

Watch lines

  2. Mia sees the sun. It's sunny.
  3. Emma sees rain. It's rainy.
  4. Toto watches leaves move. It's windy.
  5. The team sees big clouds. It's cloudy.
  6. Leo chooses a hat for a sunny day.
  7. Mia chooses boots and an umbrella for a rainy day.
  8. Emma chooses a sweater for a cloudy day.
  9. The team helps a friend choose clothes.
  10. Now it is your turn: make a Weather Choice Card.

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time

Final Project — My Weather Choice Card

Make

Make a simple card with one weather picture and one or two matching clothes pictures.

Model

  • It's rainy. Choose boots.
  • It's sunny. Choose a hat.

Your turn

Show your card. Say the weather and one clothes choice.

Practice

12 items · 3 at a time
