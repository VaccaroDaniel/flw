Teacher Guide — Adventure English World V2 Unit 11: Weather Window

Level: Pre-A1.2

Can-do: I can say simple weather words and choose clothes.

Core KP: weather words; clothes basics; It's ...; simple choosing for a friend.

Final Project: My Weather Choice Card.

Unit Overview

This unit teaches simple weather words and basic clothes choices through a warm classroom weather-window story. Learners use picture-supported chunks and make a simple weather choice card.

Lesson-by-Lesson Teaching Notes

  1. Lesson 1 — Meet the Weather: I can point to and say weather words. Use images AEW2_U011_IMG03, AEW2_U011_IMG04 and keep output short.
  2. Lesson 2 — It's Sunny / It's Rainy: I can say It's sunny and It's rainy. Use images AEW2_U011_IMG05, AEW2_U011_IMG06 and keep output short.
  3. Lesson 3 — It's Windy / It's Cloudy: I can say It's windy and It's cloudy. Use images AEW2_U011_IMG07, AEW2_U011_IMG08 and keep output short.
  4. Lesson 4 — Clothes Basics: I can name simple clothes. Use images AEW2_U011_IMG09, AEW2_U011_IMG10 and keep output short.
  5. Lesson 5 — Choose Clothes: I can choose clothes for the weather. Use images AEW2_U011_IMG11, AEW2_U011_IMG12 and keep output short.
  6. Lesson 6 — Help a Friend: I can help a friend choose clothes. Use images AEW2_U011_IMG13, AEW2_U011_IMG14, AEW2_U011_IMG15 and keep output short.
  7. Lesson 7 — Project Rehearsal: I can make a simple weather choice card. Use images AEW2_U011_IMG16, AEW2_U011_IMG17 and keep output short.

Watch Guidance

Watch video is a 54-second silent/captioned slideshow. Use it for recognition, sequence, and project preparation.

  1. Leo opens the Weather Window.
  2. Mia sees the sun. It's sunny.
  3. Emma sees rain. It's rainy.
  4. Toto watches leaves move. It's windy.
  5. The team sees big clouds. It's cloudy.
  6. Leo chooses a hat for a sunny day.
  7. Mia chooses boots and an umbrella for a rainy day.
  8. Emma chooses a sweater for a cloudy day.
  9. The team helps a friend choose clothes.
  10. Now it is your turn: make a Weather Choice Card.

Repair / Remediation Plan

  • Weather confusion: return to IMG18 and practice sunny/rainy/windy/cloudy recognition.
  • Clothes confusion: return to IMG19 and practice coat, hat, boots, T-shirt, shoes, umbrella, jacket, sweater.
  • Chunk confusion: model “It’s rainy. Choose boots.” and let learners repeat with picture support.

Project Rubric
