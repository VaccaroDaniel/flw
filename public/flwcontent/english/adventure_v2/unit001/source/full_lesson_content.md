## Lesson 1 — Say Hello
Can-do: I can hear and say Hello and Hi.
KP: Greeting recognition and production
Images: AEW2-U001-IMG-003, AEW2-U001-IMG-004

### Look
- Look at Leo and Mia.
- Leo waves. Mia smiles.

### Listen
- Hello.
- Hi.
- Hello, Mia.
- Hi, Leo.

### Say
- A: Hello.
- B: Hi.
- A: Hello, Mia.
- B: Hi, Leo.

### Try
- Point and say: Hello.
- Point and say: Hi.
- Point and say: Hello, Leo.
- Point and say: Hi, Mia.

### AI Checker
Task: Say one greeting.
Expected: Hello. or Hi.
Repair: Listen again, point to the greeting card, and repeat once.

## Lesson 7 — My Voice Greeting
Can-do: I can record a short greeting.
KP: Final greeting sequence
Images: AEW2-U001-IMG-016, AEW2-U001-IMG-017

### Look
- The learner holds a name card.
- The learner speaks.

### Listen
- Hello. I’m Leo. Goodbye.
- Hi. I’m Mia. Bye.
- Hello. I’m Emma. Goodbye.

### Say
- Use three parts:
- 1. Hello.
- 2. I’m ___.
- 3. Goodbye.

### Try
- Hold your name card.
- Look up.
- Speak clearly.
- Model: Hello. I’m Leo. Goodbye.

### AI Checker
Task: Record your voice greeting.
Expected: Hello. I’m ___. Goodbye.
Repair: Use the three-card checklist, then record again.
