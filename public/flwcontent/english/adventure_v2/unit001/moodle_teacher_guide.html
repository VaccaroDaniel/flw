Teacher Guide — Adventure English World V2 Unit 1: Hello Adventure

Unit Overview

Level: Pre-A1.1. Core KP: greetings; I’m; names; hello/goodbye. Portfolio: voice greeting + name card.

This first unit introduces a short, picture-supported communication path: Hello → I’m ___ → Goodbye. Keep all production short and supported.

Allowed KP

Hello, Hi, I’m + name, What’s your name?, Goodbye, Bye, Leo, Mia, Emma, Toto.

Blocked KP

No colors, numbers, age, countries, family, likes/dislikes, full grammar tables, he/she, spelling tests, or independent writing.

Target output

Audio: “Hello. I’m ___. Goodbye.” Card: learner name + I’m ___.

Lesson-by-Lesson Notes

LessonGoalTeaching noteRepair
1 — Say HelloI can hear and say Hello and Hi.Use image first, then audio model, then Practice block. Keep output at word/phrase level.Listen again, point to the greeting card, and repeat once.
2 — I’m LeoI can say a name with I’m.Use image first, then audio model, then Practice block. Keep output at word/phrase level.Repeat the model, then try again.
3 — What’s Your Name?I can ask and answer a name question with help.Use image first, then audio model, then Practice block. Keep output at word/phrase level.Put the question card before the answer card, then retry.
4 — Hi and ByeI can choose a greeting when someone comes or goes.Use image first, then audio model, then Practice block. Keep output at word/phrase level.Sort picture cards into meet and leave, then retry.
5 — Meet the TeamI can greet Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto.Use image first, then audio model, then Practice block. Keep output at word/phrase level.Match the name card to the character, then retry.
6 — My Name CardI can make a simple name card.Use image first, then audio model, then Practice block. Keep output at word/phrase level.Compare with the model card and add the missing part.
7 — My Voice GreetingI can record a short greeting.Use image first, then audio model, then Practice block. Keep output at word/phrase level.Use the three-card checklist, then record again.
