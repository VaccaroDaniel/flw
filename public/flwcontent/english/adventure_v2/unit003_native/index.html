Lesson 1 — Meet the Trail Numbersnumbers 0–5

Today I can: I can see and say numbers 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5.

Story

  • Toto runs to the trail.
  • Leo smiles. “Hello, Toto!”
  • Toto sees number signs.
  • Leo says, “Look! 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.”
  • Toto sees one star.
  • Toto sees two flowers.
  • Leo says, “Let’s count!”

Listen

  • 0 — zero
  • 1 — one
  • 2 — two
  • 3 — three
  • 4 — four
  • 5 — five

Say

  • How many?
  • One star.
  • Two flowers.
  • Three balls.
  • Four leaves.
  • Five apples.

Try it

Point to each sign. Count with Toto. Match each number to the picture group.

Lesson 6 — One Star, Two Starsone and more than one; plural -s noticing

Today I can: I can notice one thing and more than one thing.

Story

  • Leo shows one star.
  • Toto looks at two stars.
  • Leo says, “One star. Two stars.”
  • Mia shows one flower.
  • Emma shows four flowers.
  • The friends sort one and more than one.

Listen

  • one star
  • two stars
  • one flower
  • three flowers
  • one ball
  • four balls

Say

  • One star. Two stars.
  • One book. Two books.
  • One apple. Three apples.
  • One flower. Four flowers.

Try it

Sort picture cards into “one” and “more than one.” Notice the small -s sound in many words.

Story

  • Leo finds the number line.

  • One or more?

Say

  • Twelve stars.
  • How many flags?
  • Twenty flags.
  • Goodbye, Number Trail!

Try it

Move around the review map. Count, ask, answer, and check.

Practice

Final Project — My Number Challenge CardLearners make a simple number challenge card with 2–4 counted picture groups and short oral lines.

Make

  • Choose two, three, or four numbers from 0–20.
  • Draw simple object groups: stars, balls, flowers, books, apples, bags, or pencils.
  • Write or place the matching numerals.
  • Ask “How many?” for one picture group.
  • Say the mini project script.

Project Model

  • Hello. I’m ___.
  • How many stars? Three stars.
  • How many balls? Five balls.
  • How many flowers? Two flowers.
  • Goodbye.

Success Check

  • I used numbers.
  • I used pictures.
  • I asked “How many?”
  • I said the answer.
  • I said hello and goodbye.
