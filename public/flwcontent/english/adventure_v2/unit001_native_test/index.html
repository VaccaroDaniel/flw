Lesson 1

Say Hello

Open / close
✅ I can hear and say Hello and Hi.

Look

  • Look at Leo and Mia.
  • Leo waves. Mia smiles.

Listen

  • Hello.
  • Hi.
  • Hello, Mia.
  • Hi, Leo.

Say

  • A: Hello.
  • B: Hi.
  • A: Hello, Mia.
  • B: Hi, Leo.

Try

  • Point and say: Hello.
  • Point and say: Hi.
  • Point and say: Hello, Leo.
  • Point and say: Hi, Mia.

AI Checker

Task: Say one greeting.

Say / show: Hello. or Hi.

Feedback: Clear voice, friendly tone, and clear /h/ sound.

Repair: Listen again, point to the greeting card, and repeat once.

AI Checker

My Voice Greeting

Open / close
✅ I can record a short greeting.

Look

  • The learner holds a name card.
  • The learner speaks.

Listen

  • Hello. I’m Leo. Goodbye.
  • Hi. I’m Mia. Bye.
  • Hello. I’m Emma. Goodbye.

Say

  • Use three parts:
  • 1. Hello.
  • 2. I’m ___.
  • 3. Goodbye.

Try

  • Hold your name card.
  • Look up.
  • Speak clearly.
  • Model: Hello. I’m Leo. Goodbye.

AI Checker

Task: Record your voice greeting.

Say / show: Hello. I’m ___. Goodbye.

Feedback: Greeting, I’m + name, goodbye, clear voice, and friendly tone.

Repair: Use the three-card checklist, then record again.
