Unit Opener: Family Tree Mission+

Start

Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto find photo cards. They make a friendly tree poster. You can use family cards or friend cards.

This is my mom. / This is my friend. / This is Toto.

Your turn: Point to one card and say one line.

🔊

Repair hint: Use a card first, then choose one short line.

Final Project: My Family/Friend Poster — I can make and present a poster.+

Make it

Make a poster with 3 or 4 cards. Use family cards, friend cards, or Toto. Say your lines.

This is my mom. / This is my friend. / This is Toto.
1. Choose 3 or 4 cards.
2. Use a short line.
3. Show your poster.
4. Use a clear voice and smile.

🔊

Repair hint: Check: 3 lines, clear voice, smile. Use the phrase bank if needed.
Portfolio evidence: Poster + voice lines
