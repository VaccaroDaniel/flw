Teacher Guide

Unit 10 Family Tree

Course: Adventure English World V2 · Level: Pre-A1.2

Can-do: I can introduce family or friends with simple phrases.

Unit Overview

Main situation: Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto make a friendly family/friend tree for show-and-tell.

Core KP: family/friend words; fixed chunks: This is my ___., Who’s this?, It’s my ___.

Final project: My Family/Friend Poster. Learners make a poster with 3 or 4 cards and say 3–4 short lines.

Blocked KP: his/her/our/their, possessive ’s, he/she grammar, has got, there is/are, family biography, address/age/nationality/family history.

Teaching Notes

  • Keep production short and safe: one card + one line first.
  • Offer family cards, friend cards, or Toto so learners can avoid private family disclosure.
  • Use choral repetition for This is my, then card-word substitution.
  • For errors, point to the phrase bank rather than explaining grammar.
  • For the project, assess confidence, clarity, and correct chunk use, not family detail.
