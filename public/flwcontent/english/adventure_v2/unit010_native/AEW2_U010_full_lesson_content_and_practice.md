# Adventure English World V2 — Unit 10 Family Tree

**Course:** Adventure English World V2
**Unit Number:** 10
**Unit Code:** AEW2_U010
**Unit Title:** Family Tree
**CEFR / FLW Band:** Pre-A1.2
**Learner Age:** Children about 7–8
**Can-do Goal:** I can introduce family or friends with simple phrases.
**Main Situation:** Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto make a friendly family/friend tree for show-and-tell.
**Core KP:** family/friend words; fixed chunks: This is my ___., Who's this?, It's my ___.
**Final Project:** My Family/Friend Poster
**Practice Target:** 108 on-page Practice items: Lessons 1–7 = 84, Watch = 12, Project = 12
**Allowed KP:** mom, dad, sister, brother, grandma, grandpa, friend, dog, family tree, photo card; This is my ___; Who's this?; It's my ___.
**Review KP:** my/your recognition; hello; colors/numbers 1–5; look/listen/point/draw instructions
**Blocked KP:** his/her/our/their, possessive 's, he/she grammar, has got, there is/are, family biography, address/age/nationality/family history
