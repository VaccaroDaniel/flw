Toy Box Mystery

I can name toys and say where something is.

Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto find toys and solve a gentle toy-box mystery.

Key words: ball, car, doll, robot, kite, teddy bear, blocks, train. Key phrases: Where is…? · It’s in/on/under…

Unit Opener: Toy Box Mystery+

Start

Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto find a toy box. A toy is missing. They look, point, and listen. Where is the toy?

toy box / Where is the toy? / It's in the box.

Your turn: Point to the toy box and say: toy box.

🔊

Repair hint: Use the picture first. Say one toy word, then one place word.

Lesson 5: Where Is the Toy? — I can ask and answer Where is the toy?+

Study

The team asks about toys in the room. Use a short question and answer.

A: Where is the robot? B: It's in the box. / A: Where is the car? B: It's on the shelf. / A: Where is the ball? B: It's under the table.

🔊

Your turn: Ask one friend: Where is the toy? Answer with one location.

Repair hint: Question: Where is the ___? Answer: It's ___ the ___.

Final Project: Toy Location Page — I can show toys and say where they are.+

Make it

Choose three toys. Put each toy in a place: in the box, on the shelf, or under the table. Show your page.

Where is the ball? It's in the box. / Where is the robot? It's on the shelf. / Where is the teddy bear? It's under the table.
1. Choose 3 or 4 toys.
2. Put each toy in, on, or under.
3. Say a short sentence.
4. Show your page.

🔊

Repair hint: Choose toy + choose place + say the location sentence.
Portfolio evidence: Toy Location Page + short presentation
