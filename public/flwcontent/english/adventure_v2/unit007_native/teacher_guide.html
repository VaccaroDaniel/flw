Teacher Guide

Unit 7 Toy Box Mystery

Course: Adventure English World V2 · Level: Pre-A1.2

Can-do: I can name toys and say where something is.

Unit Overview

Main situation: Leo, Mia, Emma, and Toto find toys and solve a gentle toy-box mystery.

Core KP: toy words; in/on/under; Where is…?; It’s…

Final project: My Toy Location Page. Learners show 3 or 4 toys and say where they are.

Blocked KP: there is/are, behind/next to/between, possessives, long room descriptions, toy prices, family language, colors as grammar focus.

Teaching Notes

  • Keep production short: toy word + place word first.
  • Use pointing and real classroom objects before asking learners to speak.
  • Contrast in/on/under with gestures, not grammar explanation.
  • For wrong answers, send learners back to the matching picture.
  • For the project, assess location meaning and confidence, not long sentences.
