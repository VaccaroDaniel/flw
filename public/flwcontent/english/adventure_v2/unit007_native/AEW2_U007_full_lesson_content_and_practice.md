**Level:** Pre-A1.2

**Core KP:** toy words; prepositions in/on/under; fixed chunks: Where is the ___? It's in/on/under the ___.  
**Final Project:** My Toy Location Page

## Unit Overview
- **Course:** Adventure English World V2
- **Unit Number:** 7
- **Unit Code:** AEW2_U007
- **Unit Title:** Toy Box Mystery
- **CEFR / FLW Band:** Pre-A1.2
- **Learner Age:** Children about 7–8

### Lesson 2 — In the Box
**Can-do:** I can say where a toy is with in.

### Lesson 5 — Where Is the Toy?
**Can-do:** I can ask and answer Where is the toy?
**Images:** IMG11, IMG12
**Input:** The team asks about toys in the room. Use a short question and answer.
**Model Language:** A: Where is the robot? B: It's in the box. / A: Where is the car? B: It's on the shelf. / A: Where is the ball? B: It's under the table.
**Try It / Output:** Ask one friend: Where is the toy? Answer with one location.

**Audio Placeholder:** u007_l5_where_is_dialogue.mp3

### Lesson 6 — Toto's Toy Hunt
**Can-do:** I can follow toy clues with in, on, and under.
**Images:** IMG13, IMG14, IMG15
**Input:** Toto gives clue cards. Find the toy: in the box, on the shelf, or under the table.
**Model Language:** Clue: under. Where is the ball? It's under the table. / Clue: in. It's in the box.
**Try It / Output:** Choose one clue card and say the toy location.
**Practice IDs:** AEW2-U007-L6-Q01 to AEW2-U007-L6-Q12
**Audio Placeholder:** u007_l6_toy_hunt.mp3
**Repair Hint:** Look at the clue word first: in, on, or under.

### Lesson 7 — Say and Show
**Can-do:** I can prepare my toy location page.
**Images:** IMG16, IMG17
**Input:** Make a Toy Location Page. Choose three toys and three places. Practice one question and one answer.
**Model Language:** Where is the ball? It's in the box. / Where is the car? It's on the shelf. / Where is the robot? It's under the table.
**Try It / Output:** Show your page and say three toy locations.
**Practice IDs:** AEW2-U007-L7-Q01 to AEW2-U007-L7-Q12
**Audio Placeholder:** u007_l7_project_rehearsal.mp3
**Repair Hint:** Use the same frame for each toy: Where is the ___? It's ___ the ___.
