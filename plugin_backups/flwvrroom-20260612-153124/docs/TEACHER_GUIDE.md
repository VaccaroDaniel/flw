# Teacher Guide: FLW VR Room MVP

## Lesson idea

**Title:** A1 At the Café  
**Mission:** Order one drink politely.

## Student task

1. Click the waiter.
2. Click the menu.
3. Click the cup.
4. Click the table.
5. Click the cashier.
6. Answer the quiz.
7. Complete the mission.

## Knowledge points

```text
A1-VOC-FOOD-001: coffee / tea / water
A1-FUNC-ORDER-001: ordering politely
A1-LIS-QUESTION-001: What would you like?
A1-SPK-REPLY-001: I’d like...
```

## Score model

```text
Waiter: 20
Menu: 15
Cup: 15
Table: 10
Cashier: 15
Quiz: 25
Total: 100
Passing: 70
```

## Moodle tracking

The plugin saves the attempt and updates the Moodle gradebook. Completion is marked when all room objects and the quiz are completed.
